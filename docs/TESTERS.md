# Testing ThoughtSpace: start here

Thanks for trying it. This takes about **15 minutes**. You need a Windows 10 or 11 PC and a microphone (a laptop's built-in one is fine).

Nothing you write or say leaves your computer unless you send it. Your map stays on your computer, and your voice is transcribed on your computer. The developers only see what you choose to put in the feedback form at the end.

## 1. Install (2 min)
1. Download **Thoughtspace-x.y.z-Setup.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Ronak1Nambiar/thoughtspace/releases/latest).
2. Windows will say it "protected your PC", because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Pick a look, keep or change the assistant's name, and connect a model. **Sign in with OpenRouter** is the quickest (a free account works). If you already use ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini with an API key, paste that instead. If you have Ollama, pick it.

## 2. Put a few things on the map (5 min)
1. Type one thing on your mind in **What's on your mind?** and press **Ctrl+Enter**.
2. Click **Start conversation** and talk for 20–30 seconds about two or three things you're juggling. Pause when you're done, and it replies.
3. Look at the map. Each idea should be its own card.

## 3. Talk about something that's already there (4 min)
1. Start a conversation again and ask about one of those cards ("how should I handle the …?").
2. The map should glide to that card and light it up. The assistant's next steps appear around it as **dashed items**.
3. Click an item, then **Keep item** or **Remove**. Use **Add an item…** to add one of your own.
4. Click empty space on the map to go back to the full view.

## 4. Poke at it (2 min)
- Drag cards around. Select one and **Discuss** it by typing.
- Change the reply voice with the dropdown next to **Start conversation**.
- Close the app and open it again. Everything should still be there.

## 5. Tell us how it went (2 min)
In the app, open **Help → Send feedback**. It's the same five questions, plus a few usage numbers counted on your computer (days opened, thoughts captured, whether a model is connected). You can edit or delete any of it. Press **Copy feedback** and open it as a GitHub issue if you have an account. Short answers are perfect. You can also use the **[feedback form](https://github.com/Ronak1Nambiar/thoughtspace/issues/new?template=feedback.yml)** on GitHub. Anything broken goes in **[Something broke](https://github.com/Ronak1Nambiar/thoughtspace/issues/new?template=bug.yml)**, ideally with a screenshot.

**Then, the real test:** if you find yourself opening it again next week without being asked, that's the best feedback there is.

The app updates itself, so you'll get fixes without reinstalling.
